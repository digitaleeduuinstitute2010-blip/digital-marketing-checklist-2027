# Digital Marketing Checklist 2027

A free, practical checklist for beginners and working marketers. It covers SEO, AEO/GEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads, so you can audit a website or campaign step by step.

Maintained by the trainers at [99EEDU](https://99eedu.com/), Kolkata.

---

## Who is this for?

- Students learning digital marketing
- Freelancers and small business owners
- Marketers who want a quick audit list

---

## 1. SEO Basics Checklist

- [ ] Every page has a unique title tag (under 60 characters)
- [ ] Every page has a meta description (under 160 characters)
- [ ] One H1 per page, with logical H2/H3 headings
- [ ] Site loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Site is mobile-friendly and uses HTTPS
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted in Google Search Console
- [ ] No broken links or duplicate pages
- [ ] Images are compressed and have alt text
- [ ] Internal links connect related pages

## 2. AEO / GEO Checklist (Answer and AI Search)

- [ ] Each key page gives a short, direct answer near the top
- [ ] FAQ section with real customer questions
- [ ] Schema markup added (FAQ, Organization, LocalBusiness)
- [ ] Author names and credentials shown on articles
- [ ] Facts, statistics and sources are accurate and up to date
- [ ] Brand is mentioned consistently across trusted sites
- [ ] Content covers the topic fully, not just one keyword

> Learn more: [SEO vs GEO vs AEO explained](https://99eedu.com/answer-engine-optimization/)

## 3. Local SEO Checklist

- [ ] Google Business Profile is claimed and verified
- [ ] Name, address and phone are identical everywhere
- [ ] Photos are added and updated regularly
- [ ] Reviews are collected and answered
- [ ] Location pages exist for each service area

## 4. Google Ads Checklist

- [ ] Conversion tracking tested (forms, calls, WhatsApp)
- [ ] Keywords are based on buying intent
- [ ] Negative keywords added (free, jobs, salary and similar)
- [ ] Ads match the landing page promise
- [ ] Location and schedule settings are correct
- [ ] Landing page is fast and mobile-friendly
- [ ] Form is short, with a clear call to action

> Learn more: [Why Google Ads get clicks but no leads](https://99eedu.com/why-your-google-ads-get-clicks-but-no-leads-and-fixes/)

## 5. Meta Ads Checklist

- [ ] Meta Pixel and Conversions API working
- [ ] Events verified in Events Manager
- [ ] Clear audience and objective chosen
- [ ] Creative tested in at least 3 variations
- [ ] Lead form or landing page tested on mobile
- [ ] Budget and learning phase respected before edits

> Learn more: [How to generate leads with Meta Ads](https://99eedu.com/how-to-generate-leads-with-meta-ads-step-by-step/)

## 6. Analytics Checklist

- [ ] Google Analytics 4 installed through Google Tag Manager
- [ ] Key events and conversions defined
- [ ] Search Console linked to Analytics
- [ ] Weekly report: traffic, leads, cost per lead
